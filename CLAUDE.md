# Project instructions

## LinkedIn work
For any LinkedIn-related request (posts, comments, replies, reposts, content plans, profile, hooks, analytics), use only the `linkedin-skills` plugin (sergebulaev/linkedin-skills). Pick the matching skill:

- New post: `linkedin-skills:linkedin-post-writer`
- Review / de-AI a draft: `linkedin-skills:linkedin-humanizer` (`--mode audit` for audits)
- Turn other content into a post: `linkedin-skills:linkedin-repurposer`
- Weekly / monthly plan: `linkedin-skills:linkedin-content-planner`
- Comment on or repost someone's post: `linkedin-skills:linkedin-comment-drafter`
- Reply to comments: `linkedin-skills:linkedin-reply-handler`
- Break down a viral post's hook: `linkedin-skills:linkedin-hook-extractor`
- Profile rewrite: `linkedin-skills:linkedin-profile-optimizer`
- Gather story material: `linkedin-skills:linkedin-interviewer`
- Engager / thread tracking: `linkedin-skills:linkedin-engager-analytics`, `linkedin-skills:linkedin-thread-monitor`
- Team advocacy programs: `linkedin-skills:linkedin-employee-advocacy`

Don't use other LinkedIn or copywriting skills (e.g. `anthropic-skills:linkedin-post-writer`, `anthropic-skills:linkedin-copywriter`) for LinkedIn tasks.

## Brand and compliance
- Brand name: Synova. Mutual fund content must follow AMFI and SEBI guidelines: no guaranteed or implied returns, label illustrations with their assumptions, no specific scheme recommendations, include the standard AMFI disclaimer, and add Synova's ARN once it's available.
