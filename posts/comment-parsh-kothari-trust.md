---
Option A (T2, the boring years)

Parsh, in personal finance most of the trust gets built in the boring years. Anyone sounds smart when markets are up. What people remember is who picked up the phone in a bad month and told them to sit tight, or who said "don't buy this" when selling it would've been easier.

---
Option B (T2, the uncomfortable thing)

For me it's saying the uncomfortable thing early, Parsh. In money conversations that's usually "you're taking more risk than you think" or "this won't do what you're hoping". People forgive a bad year in the markets. They don't forget being told only what they wanted to hear.
